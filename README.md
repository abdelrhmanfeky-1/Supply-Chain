# 📊 Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

The **Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI solution designed to provide a comprehensive view of supply chain performance and operational efficiency.

The dashboard analyzes key business metrics across **products, suppliers, locations, transportation modes, shipping performance, sales, costs, profitability, and product quality**. It transforms raw supply chain data into meaningful visual insights that help stakeholders monitor performance, identify operational issues, and make data-driven decisions.

The solution combines **Python, Power Query, DAX, and Power BI** to build an end-to-end data analytics workflow, starting from data preparation and cleaning through data modeling, analysis, visualization, and business insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objective of the dashboard is to provide a centralized analytical view of the supply chain and answer important business questions such as:

* How much revenue is being generated?
* What are the total shipping costs?
* Which suppliers generate the highest revenue?
* Which product types perform best?
* Which locations have the strongest sales performance?
* Which transportation modes have the highest shipping times?
* What is the average product defect rate?
* What are the inspection results across the supply chain?
* How does quantity sold change across orders?
* Which areas contribute most to revenue and profitability?
* Where can supply chain performance be improved?

## 💰 Revenue Analysis

The dashboard analyzes revenue across different dimensions to identify the main revenue drivers.

### Revenue by Product Type

The dashboard compares revenue generated by different product categories, including:

* Skincare
* Haircare
* Cosmetics

This analysis helps identify the product types contributing the most to overall revenue.

### Revenue by Supplier

Supplier performance is analyzed based on the revenue generated by each supplier.

This allows stakeholders to:

* Identify high-performing suppliers
* Compare supplier contribution
* Evaluate supplier importance
* Identify potential opportunities for supplier optimization

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

### Power BI

Used to create the interactive dashboard, data model, KPIs, and visualizations.

### Power Query

Used for data transformation and preparation before loading the data into the Power BI model.

### DAX

Used to create calculated measures and dynamic KPIs for revenue, cost, quantity, profitability, defect rate, and other performance indicators.

### Python

Used for data cleaning, preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, and supporting the analytical workflow.

### Pandas & NumPy

Used for data manipulation, transformation, and numerical analysis.

### Matplotlib & Seaborn

Used for exploratory data visualization and identifying patterns and relationships within the dataset.

### Microsoft Excel

Used as a source and/or intermediate format for the supply chain dataset.
